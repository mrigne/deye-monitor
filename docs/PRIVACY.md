# Privacy Policy — Deye Monitor

Deye Monitor does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

- The app connects only to the solar inverter data logger whose IP address you
  enter, on your own local network.
- The inverter IP address, logger serial number and your display preferences
  are stored only on your device.
- There is no analytics, advertising, tracking, account or cloud service.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mrigne/deye-monitor/issues
